### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/primer/octicons/main/icons/search-16.svg" width="20" height="20" /> Why Application Logs are Vital for Troubleshooting

Application logs are vital for troubleshooting because they record chronological timestamps, client IP addresses, specific requested URIs, and exact HTTP status codes, enabling cloud engineers to precisely isolate when, where, and why failures occur without guessing.
